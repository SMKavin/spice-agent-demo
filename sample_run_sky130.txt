### How the design was obtained  

1. **First estimate (square‑law / gm‑Id)**  
   - Desired gain ≈ 10 – 12 V/V → gm·RD ≈ 10 – 12.  
   - Picked a convenient RD = 20 kΩ → needed gm ≈ 0.5 – 0.6 mS.  
   - To keep the supply current ≤ 100 µA, Id ≈ 45 µA was targeted, which gives gm ≈ 2·Id/Vov ≈ 0.5 mS for a modest over‑drive Vov ≈ 0.2 V.  
   - Using the sky130 1.8‑V NMOS with L = 0.5 µm, a width of about **12 µm** (W/L ≈ 24) gives the required Id at a gate bias near 0.7 V.

2. **Simulation‑based tuning**  
   - **Run 1:** W = 2 µm → gain too low, Vout too high.  
   - **Run 2:** W = 15 µm → Vout in range but gain ≈ 13.5 V/V (above spec).  
   - **Run 3:** Reduced to W = 12 µm, Vbias = 0.7 V → Vout ≈ 1.02 V (slightly high).  
   - **Run 4:** Increased Vbias to 0.75 V → Vout fell to ≈ 0.39 V (too low).  
   - **Run 5:** Adjusted Vbias to 0.72 V → Vout ≈ 0.78 V (just below lower limit) and gain ≈ 12.7 V/V (still a bit high).  
   - **Run 6 (final):** Lowered the load resistor to **RD = 18 kΩ** while keeping W = 12 µm and Vbias = 0.72 V. This moved Vout into the 0.8‑1.0 V band and reduced the gain to **≈ 11.7 V/V**, comfortably inside the required 10‑12 range.
